Lotus Grove Sanctuary & Transparency

Mobile UI & UX Concept Exploration for Devotee Transparency & Monastery Expense Tracking

1. Sanctuary Home

Devotee landing experience with daily teachings, designated cause progress, and giving CTA.

  • Warm rice-paper palette with saffron accents
  • Active Cause Funds: Food/Alms, Medicine, Utilities
  • Verified Trust Badges on each active fund
  • Persistent bottom navigation with 5 sacred tabs

2. Transparency Ledger

Searchable timeline of itemized expenses with full receipt bottom-sheet inspection.

  • Filter Chips: All, Alms Food, Medicine, Power, Repairs
  • Itemized receipt viewer with zoom & details
  • Official Seal: "Verified by Monastery Steward"
  • Radical clarity: Every cent traced to its purpose

3. Book of Intentions (Prayer Wall)

Community space where devotees' prayer dedications live and receive monks' blessings.

  • Intentions Filter: Memorial, Healing, Peace, My Prayers
  • Monk Blessing Stamp: Golden lotus verified badge
  • Community Merit: "Rejoice in Merit" (🙏 Anumodana)
  • Privacy options: Family name or Anonymous

4. UTXO Transparency Ledger

Blockchain-style fund flow: Donations as inputs, spending & change as outputs, with personal TX tracker.

  • Inputs (Donations): Verified donor TX hashes & amounts
  • Outputs (Spending): Merchant payment + unspent change
  • Provenance Tracker: Devotee enters TX hash to see exact % spent
  • Receipt Proof: Cryptographic hash linked to physical receipt

5. Monk Steward Portal

Mobile admin console for logging expenses and reciting devotee prayer dedications.

  • Treasury Balance with real-time low-fund alerts
  • One-Tap Expense Logger with camera receipt capture
  • Morning Chanting Queue: Devotee prayer intentions
  • Recite & Bless Action with gold lotus stamp
